About this guide

About

What we're about

We're all about supporting the independent shows and venues. Atlanta doesn't get enough recognition nor support for the fantastic comedic talent that it has. We're here to change that.

Where listings come from

Listings here come from web searches, gossipy neighbors, word of mouth, the rumor mill, crystal balls, and many other sources.
